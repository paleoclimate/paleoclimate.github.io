# Basin outlines recede and the coastline stays dark

Gabriel asked the basin lines to sit back: a weak gray, and transparent, so a toggled limit clutters the climate reading less. The coastline should stay the ordinary black shore. Basin outlines are slate-400 (`#94a3b8`) at opacity 0.40. The weight stays 1.35 px, so the contour is still a line you can trace once the Basins layer is on; the quiet comes from the gray and the transparency, not from a thinner stroke. The coastline returns to the dark shore this atlas published before the faint-coast trial: slate-700 (`#334155`), 1.05 px, opacity 0.88. That stroke is what “preta normal” means here. Layer order is unchanged: raster, then the dark coastline, then the faint basin outlines, then the markers. The vector PDF uses the same path styles.

**Considered options:** (1) this pair; (2) leave the coastline faint and only recolor the basins; (3) pure black (`#000000`) at full opacity, or the older shore `#1b2733` at 1.1 px and opacity 0.85; (4) the faint-coast trial on the basins (slate-500, 0.8 px, opacity 0.38); (5) thin the basin stroke as well as lightening it. (2) leaves both lines quiet, and the request was for the shore to stay the black reference. (3) is blacker than the shore this atlas already called normal. (4) disappeared on the humid and semi-arid classes when it was tried on the coastline. (5) was not asked; the clutter was the wine colour (`#6b2d4a` at opacity 0.92), not the contour weight.
