# The plus section stacks each note under its label

Paleoenvironment, Dating Evidence and Lithology shared the popup's two-column grid. The label column took the width of those words, and the note was left a couple of characters wide, so it broke every word or two. Those three fields now stack: the label on its own line, the note across the card. The card also has a minimum width of 260px, so a long lithology reads as a paragraph inside the 280px cap. N/A stays on one line. Basin, country, climate, age and reference stay in the short two-column list.

**Considered options:** (1) stack the three notes; (2) keep the two-column grid and only shorten the labels. (2) still spends a column on the label beside a paragraph that needs the full width.
