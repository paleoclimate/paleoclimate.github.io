# A point popup stays a short card and scrolls

Opening the plus on a data point appended paleoenvironment, dating evidence and lithology under the citation. A long lithology note grew the card past the map. The card now stops at 280px and the rest scrolls inside it. A blank cell still reads N/A, and that token stays on one line: the value column keeps enough width for it, and the letters do not break at the slash. Longer notes still wrap on word boundaries.

**Considered options:** (1) cap the card and scroll; (2) let the card grow with the note; (3) give the card a constant height even when the plus is closed. (2) is what made the note impossible to read against the map. (3) would leave an empty tall card on a point whose fields are short.
