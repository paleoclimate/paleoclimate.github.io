# 145 Ma stays in this candidate

145 Ma has no conceptual points and no paleozone file, and it may be dropped later for lack of evidence. This round applies the same condensation and indicator IDW to every age, including 145 Ma and 115 Ma.

**Considered options:** (1) publish all seventeen ages with one rule; (2) suppress 145 Ma now. Suppression was left as a possibility, not as this delivery. The group asked for the change on every age.

The viewer lists 145 Ma again. The age is still generated. See `docs/adr/0042-viewer-lists-145-ma.md`.

145 Ma now has a conceptual lattice at `conceptual/145_ma_conceptual.geojson`. The class is stored in `Paleozona`, not `Climate_Cl`. See `docs/adr/0041-conceptual-files-anchor-interpolation.md`.
