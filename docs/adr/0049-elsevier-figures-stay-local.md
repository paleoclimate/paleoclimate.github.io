# Hay & Floegel (2012) figures stay out of the repository

The Floegel comparison and the other Hay & Floegel stages (`docs/adr/0048-hay-floegel-stages-scored-by-shoreline-fit.md`) read crops of Figs. 1–7 of Hay & Floegel (2012), Earth-Science Reviews 115, 262–272. The paper is © Elsevier and not open access. Elsevier is a signatory of the STM Permissions Guidelines, which allow up to three figures from one article in a new scholarly work published by another signatory publisher; a public code repository and the project website are neither, so republishing the figures there needs a licence (RightsLink). The repository is public and its site is protected only by a shared password.

**Decision:** the scans, crops and anything that shows the figure itself stay local and are ignored by git: `COMPARISON/inputs/*_floegel.png`, `*_hay.png`, `*_chumakov.png`, `legenda.png`, `COMPARISON/warped_floegel_*.png` and `COMPARISON/gcp_picker.html` (it embeds the scans). What stays committed is ours: the control points (`gcp_*.json`), the georeference of the Chumakov crops (`chumakov_georef.json`), the reference renders of our raster, and the reports. The reports no longer embed the warped figure for this atlas; they show the three-class reduction on our grid and a note with the source. Burgener et al. (2023) figures come from Scotese et al. (2024), CC BY 4.0, and are kept, with attribution (`ATLAS_IMAGE_SHAREABLE`).

**Considered options:** (1) keep the crops in git; (2) keep them local; (3) request a RightsLink licence. (1) is the risk above. (3) costs time and money and would cover a limited period of web use. (2) keeps the comparison reproducible for anyone with the PDF: `compare_chumakov.py extract` rebuilds the Chumakov crops; the 105 and 115 Ma crops were cut by hand, so their owners keep them locally (they are also in the history until it is cleaned).

**Consequences:** removing the files from git also deletes them from a working copy when it pulls this change, so copy `COMPARISON/inputs/` aside first, or restore them afterwards with `git show <old commit>:COMPARISON/inputs/105_floegel.png`. Earlier commits still hold the scans and reports with the embedded figure; clean them with the same history rewrite planned for `CONDENSED/` and `conceptual/`. A test fails if any of these files becomes tracked again.
