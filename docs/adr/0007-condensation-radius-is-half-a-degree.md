# Condensation radius is half a degree

The 1.0° candidate glued too many citations into one vote. Humid rows outnumbered the others inside those groups, so dry anchors reaching the IDW became scarce. Gabriel asked to halve the radius and look at the maps before any other change.

Data points now cluster at 0.5° Euclidean distance, still in source coordinates, still transitive, still one row one vote. Power stays 1. The snap disk stays off. A cap on how many points may join one group, and a later move to power 2, are not in this run.

**Considered options:** (1) keep 1.0°; (2) 0.5° now, and only then decide whether to go to 0.25° or cap the group size; (3) raise power to 2 in the same run. (2) is what he asked to see on video first.
