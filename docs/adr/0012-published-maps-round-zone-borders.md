# Published maps round the class-zone borders

Gabriel accepted the KNN + IDW maps at power 4 and 0.5° and asked whether the zones could be rounder and less square. The squareness comes from the anchors: conceptual points sit on a grid about 3° apart, and at power 4 the winning class follows that grid, so zones come out as crosses, staircases and right angles. Lowering the power to 3 kept the crosses.

The class raster now blurs each class's share of the IDW weight with a Gaussian of sigma 2.0°, then takes the class with the greatest blurred share. An anchor whose cell won its own class before the blur keeps it after: around that anchor the blur is blended down to 1.0°, 0.5° and finally none. A fixed blur with no such step erased small islands, up to 17 of 299 anchors at 100 Ma, and the narrow dry belt at 145 Ma. Restoring a disc around the lost anchor brought them back as a ring around the marker. With the blend, no anchor loses its class at any of the six ages tried (70, 85, 100, 115, 130, 145 Ma). About 2 to 3% of cells change class. Mean macro IoU against the paleozones on 100, 120 and 130 Ma, scored the same way for every sigma, went from 0.545 unsmoothed to 0.554 at 1.0°, 0.562 at 1.5°, 0.565 at 2.0° and 0.571 at 2.5°.

Power stays 4, the condensation radius stays 0.5°, KNN stays at 8 neighbors. `--edge-smooth` sets the sigma; 0 turns it off. Published files carry it in the name: `_power4.0_smooth2.0_class-thirds_kdtree`. Markers stay on the original citation class. The overlay is still drawn from the GeoTIFF, which now holds the rounded classes.

**Considered options:** (1) lower the power; (2) fixed blur of the class shares, sigma 0.75°, 1.5°, 2.0° or 2.5°; (3) the same blur with a disc restored around each lost anchor; (4) the blur stepped down around each lost anchor. (4) at 2.0° is the run. (1) changes every zone's extent and keeps the grid shapes. (2) erases islands. (3) leaves rings. 2.5° is a little rounder and scores 0.006 higher, but it changes about one more percent of cells and shrinks the large dry zones at 85 and 115 Ma, which is more than he asked for.
